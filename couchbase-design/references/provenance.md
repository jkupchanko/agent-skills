# Provenance and portability

This is a personal reusable design profile distilled from John's reviewed Couchbase calculator, with calculator preferences from 2026-11-06 and hotel-demo refinements through 2026-11-08. It is not endorsed as the official Couchbase design system. Current instructions and current evidence take precedence over defaults.

## Visual reference sources

- https://www.couchbase.com/database-tco-calculator/ — font sizes, red/cream palette, control states; selected checkbox measured as #21EA2A, 46px, 4px black border, 16px radius, bold SVG tick.
- https://www.couchbase.com/products/ai-data-plane/ — visual family and typography reference.
- https://www.couchbase.com/ — logo source.
- https://couchbase-ai-savings.vercel.app/ — approved demo example; may evolve after this snapshot.

Bundled logos originate from the existing authorized calculator assets. Couchbase marks remain Couchbase's property. The dark logo changes wordmark fill only. Favicon extracts the existing couch symbol. No proprietary font or official bitmap illustration is redistributed by this skill.

## Related skill ideas incorporated

- https://github.com/simota/agent-skills/tree/main/palette — cognitive load, feedback, state-based usability evaluation. Palette is not primarily a color picker.
- https://github.com/AntJanus/skillbox/tree/main/skills/color-system — semantic color roles, color harmony, separate light/dark values, contrast validation.
- Locally available Impeccable, frontend-design, apple-design, ui-ux-pro-max and web-design-guidelines informed the review approach. This skill does not vendor their instruction sets or require their installation.

Only task-relevant principles are consolidated. No inherited agent orchestration, journal, deployment, installation or approval rituals are imposed on every visual edit.

## Hotel-demo refinements

Direct user feedback approved compact current-action summaries, on-demand evidence, consistent typography, more visible neutral outlines, and seamless panel headers. Apple design contributed stable hierarchy and restrained feedback; frontend-design contributed intentional surface relationships and removal of arbitrary banner treatments. These are personal preferences, not newly measured official brand specifications. The activity view describes observable or scripted events, not private model reasoning.
